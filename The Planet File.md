## Keys

**Name:** The name of the planet  

**Flag:** The planet's flag  

**Pronunciation:** How to pronounce the planet's name  

**Capital:** The capital city of the planet  

**Leader:** The planet's leader or its political system  

**Power Status:** Each inhabited planet is assigned a military power ranking from 1 to 6,000,000,000. This may not align with the army size, as some ships are more advanced than others.  

**Army Size:** Total count of military personnel  

- **Interceptors:** Number of interceptors  
- **Battle Ships:** Number of battle ships (includes pocket destroyers)  
- [**Darkfighters:**](./The%20Technology%20of%20Rescueland%2C%20Computerland%20and%20it's%20surrounding%20Planets.md#darkfighters-greyfighters-and-lightfighters) Number of dark fighters (includes grey and lightfighters)  
- **Ground Force:** Number of foot soldiers

*Note: Rarer ship types have been excluded.*  

**Tech Level:** A measure of technological advancement, rated on a scale of 1 to 12 for military capability:  
1. Stone Age  
2. Medieval Age  
3. Present-Day Earth  
4. Regular transport available within the system  
5. Initial exploration of nearby systems  
6. Colonies established in nearby systems, exploring deeper into their universe (most Scifi films such as Star Wars is at this level)  
7. Technology to occasionally leave their universe  
8. Capability to regularly exit their universe  
9. Development of more dangerous, system-destroying weaponry  
10. Ship range surpasses the distance from Recua to Computerland and back  
11. Efficient bi-directional conversion of energy and matter
12. Transdimensional exploratory capabilities 

**Population:** Total number of inhabitants  

**Atmosphere:** Primary gases in the atmosphere  

**General:** Overview of pertinent information

### Rescueland Empire

**Flag:** ![](images/RescuelandEmpire_flag.png)

**Current Capital:** Rescueland

**Overview:**

Despite the name, the Rescueland Empire is not a traditional empire but a cooperative republic dedicated to mutual assistance among planets in the multiverse. Officially, it operates as a democratic entity emphasizing collective governance and shared defense. During wartime, however, authority shifts to a Commander of War with emergency powers. While the Council can offer counsel, the Commander alone decides final outcomes in military operations.

Currently, the Rescueland Empire works alongside The Rebels and Rebel 10 in a coalition opposing the 5th Army and Recola forces. Relations with The Severed are cooperative but strained due to their refusal to share Recola homeworld intelligence.

**Goals:**

- To unify diverse planets under a system of collaboration and support during times of crisis.
- To promote peace, stability, and development across the multiverse while serving as a mediator in disputes.


### Rebel 10

**Flag:** ![](images/Rebel10_flag.png)

**Current Capital:** Helucky

**Overview:**

The Rebel 10 is a faction composed of dissidents and ideologues who believe that the Rescueland Empire's governance is too relaxed and paternalistic. They comprise a diverse coalition, including former planets of the Rescueland Empire and planets that feel marginalized. Over time, the faction absorbed planets that felt marginalized by the Rescueland Empire's "coddling" policies—specifically, the practice of providing universal protection while local defensive capabilities atrophied.

However, they have made a temporary pact with Rescueland to fight the 5th army.

**Goals:**
- Reform the Rescueland Empire to restore local military autonomy
- Prevent other factions from falling into dependency

    
### The Rebels

**Overview:**

Prior to the Seperatist's ascension to power, a significant faction within the Rebel 10 broke off, diverging from the larger movement to form an independent force. This pivotal decision, though initially seen as a fracture, ultimately proved to be a salvation for these wayward rebels, as it shielded them from the Seperatist's brutal domination.
By seceding from the Rebel 10, they managed to avoid being subjugated, instead forging their own path, separate from the Seperatist's influence. As a result, they were able to establish themselves as a distinct entity, emerging into the spotlight as a lone bastion of notable resistance against the Seperatist's stranglehold.

Now they work closely alongside the Rescueland Empire and their former faction in their shared struggle against the 5th army.

**Goals:**

- Dismantle the 5th Army and Recola forces
- Restore order and revive the Rescueland Empire

### The 5th Army

**Current Capital:** Unknown (possibly mobile or hidden)

**Overview:**

The 5th Army operates alongside Recola in a coalition that has dominated galactic affairs since the Third Battle of Rescueland. Much information about their organization, origins, and true command structure remains classified or unknown.

What is known:
- They control Recola technology and forces, but their own origins predate Recola's prominence
- They possess a massive, mysterious battlestation resembling an asterisk—possibly centuries or millennia old
- Their fleet includes hacked vessels, including Recola interceptors vulnerable to signal infiltration
- Their strategic objective appears to be dominance of all systems sharing Rescueland's multiversal neighborhood

Command structure remains opaque. Some reports indicate a ruthless, cunning warlord at their apex, others suggest distributed command without singular leadership. Intelligence suggests they may be composed of multiple factions operating under unified direction.

**Goals:**

- Dominate all systems in Rescueland's multiversal neighborhood
- Expand influence beyond current territory
- Neutralize Alliance threat permanently

### The Pirate Court

**Overview:**

Emerging from the shadows of the collapsing factions, the Pirate Court formed a loosely affiliated network of marauders who flourished in the chaos. In the absence of central authority, they seized the opportunity to dominate trade routes and engage in rampant plunder, becoming a growing menace to any remnants of organized governance.

**Goals:**

To establish pirate havens across key sectors of space, enforce their rule through intimidation, and control trade to amass wealth and resources.

### The Severed

**Current Capital:** Keluha (Helucky-built base)

**Overview:**

The Severed is an independent faction formed when 5th Army troops refused to abandon the Keluha base after conquering it. Following the 717 ADOTR Recola Raids, the remaining 10 divisions captured the Helucky-built base from 38 Helucky defenders in what appeared an impossible victory. When the 5th Army leadership ordered them to relinquish the position, these troops defied command, severing ties and declaring sovereignty.

Named for their severed connection to the 5th Army chain of command, The Severed controls Keluha's strategic base while maintaining Recola-derived technology and ship designs. They rejected authority from both the 5th Army and Recola homeworld, operating under their own military hierarchy.

Their survival owes more to intelligence leverage than military strength. The Severed possesses knowledge of Recola's hidden homeworld location—a secret the Alliance desperately wants. By withholding this information from the Alliance, they protect their former comrades. By threatening to reveal it to the Alliance if attacked, they deter the 5th Army from reprisal.

This triangular deterrence has allowed The Severed to survive despite being outnumbered by all major powers.

The Keluha Accord formalized their relationship with the Rescueland-Helucky-Rebel Alliance: the Empire pledges military aid if the 5th Army threatens the base, provides technical training for maintenance, and allows civilian coexistence—but does not receive Recola intelligence. The Severed reciprocates by allowing Alliance access to non-military zones and sharing tactical intelligence about 5th Army movements.

**Goals:**

- Maintain independent sovereignty over Keluha base
- Protect Recola from Alliance annihilation campaigns
- Preserve former 5th Army personnel and culture 
- Avoid becoming proxy for any major power's agenda

### Independent

**Overview:**

Not part of any faction.

**Goals:**

To remain completely neutral to the skirmishes between the factions.

### Rescueland (A)

**Name:** Rescueland/Recua

**Flag:** ![](images/Rescueland_flag.png)

**Pronounced:** resc-ooh-laned/r(with tongue roll)-ekk-ooh-ah

**Capital:** Non-existent

**Leader:** none

**Power Status:** 5999999997/6000000000

Army Size:
- **Interceptors:** 224 billion
- **Battle Ships:** 12763
- **Darkfighters:** 8
- **Ground Force:** 675 quadrilion

**Tech Level:** 12

**Population:** 8.9798*10^38

**Atmosphere:** Nitrogen 90%, Hydrogen 8%, Oxygen 1%

**General:** Rescueland stands out as the first of the three prominent 'superpowers', alongside Helucky and the 5th army. What makes Rescueland particularly unique is its focus on the quality and training of its military forces, setting it apart even though it possesses the smallest army among the three superpowers. 
The sheer effectiveness and elite training of Rescueland's troops allow them to compete on equal footing with larger forces. Their emphasis on teamwork and cohesion often leads to a highly disciplined fighting force capable of executing complex maneuvers under pressure.
Geographically, Rescueland is an extraordinary planet, sometimes enveloped by the largest [FFG](./The%20Technology%20of%20Rescueland%2C%20Computerland%20and%20it's%20surrounding%20Planets.md#force-field-gel-r) known in history. In the event of an invasion attempt, the invaders must strategically group and position themselves directly in front of the FFG, waiting for the shield to expire. Invaders must carefully plan their assault, as any miscalculation could result in disastrous consequences. Also, as the population is spread thin across hundreds of floating platforms, it is a colossal task to conquer all of Rescueland. 
Rescueland's infrastructure and alliances also play a significant role in its defense strategy. While they may not have the sheer number of troops that Helucky or Recola boast, their innovative use of technology, along with diplomatic relationships developed over the years, provides them with additional layers of security. 

For the few organic beings living on Rescueland, the local cuisine includes a variety of plant-based dishes, using both native and imported ingredients, primarily shipped in from [Fufe](#fufe-r). While not a dominant religion, some express devotion to the [Super Owls](./The%20Timeline%20of%20Resueland%20History.md#0-adotr-discovery-of-the-rings), holding ceremonies to invoke their blessings for protection and guidance by these seemingly supernatural beings. [Rescueland robots](./The%20Technology%20of%20Rescueland%2C%20Computerland%20and%20it's%20surrounding%20Planets.md#rescueland-robots-r) coexist in a vibrant society where FCRT children go to school, live in houses, and experience a lifecycle reminiscent of living organisms due to the effects of Rescueland Gas. This integration creates a unique blend of culture and community, leading to educational systems and social structures akin to those of organic life.

## The Fil System

The three inhabited planets in the Fil system operate in a cooperative trio, each with a unique role. Filto specializes in military production and defense, ensuring the safety and security of the system. Filna is dedicated to civilian needs, producing essential goods and services for everyday life. Meanwhile, Filcho focuses on scientific discoveries, driving technological advancements that propel the entire system forward. Together, they create a balanced and collaborative environment that fosters growth and stability across the Fil system. However, this system was under the control of the Seperatists when Recola fell, so it is now under the power of the 5th army.
